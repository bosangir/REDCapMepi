  2. Title: TBCARE demo - your student number. Purpose: Practice / Just for fun.
  3. Project creation option: Upload a REDCap project XML file; choose TBCARE_2026_REDCap_project.xml.
  4. Click Create Project.

04The TUMAINI study

Fictional. TUMAINI (Swahili for “hope”) replaces the PISTIC study used in 2025, now as a multi-centre study.

TUMAINI follows 480 girls aged 16 to 20 who report having had sex, for 12 months, to measure the prevalence and incidence of reproductive tract infections and the use of contraception. It runs at three sites. Each site enters and sees only its own participants.

Site 1 · Dar es SalaamTemeke · 160 participants
Site 2 · Mwanza160 participants
Site 3 · Mbeya160 participants

Participants follow one of three schedules (arms). The questionnaire on sexual behaviour (FSX) is self-completed on a tablet as a survey. Missed visits are recorded on a repeating form; unscheduled visits are a repeating event.

06The tasks, part by part

A summary. The PDF instructions have every detail and the arm tables.

A · Project, users and roles 5 points

  • Title TUMAINI 2026 - group XX, Practice purpose
  • Enable longitudinal and surveys
  • Add group members, tafadzwa and redcap
  • Roles: Data Manager and Site Staff

B · Instruments 25 points

  • SELIG, SEGE, FSX, FGE, MV from the CRFs
  • LOC: your own locator and knowledge form
  • Validation, identifiers, consistent codes
  • Every CRF arrow becomes branching logic

C · Arms and events 15 points

  • Standard, Intensive, Reduced
  • Day offsets and windows as given
  • Designate instruments per event
  • [arm-number] hides FGE items in arm 3

D · Repeating 10 points

  • Unscheduled: repeating event
  • mv: repeating instrument in Missed visits
  • Custom label that pipes the visit

E · Data Access Groups NEW 15 points

  • Three DAGs, one per site and group member
  • Auto-numbering with the DAG prefix
  • 18 fake participants, plus 3 imported per site
  • Prove that sites cannot see each other

F · Advanced features 20 points

  • Field embedding (signs and lab tables)
  • Piping (Of these [sex_n] times…)
  • Calculations, a validation warning, action tags
  • FSX as a survey, answered as a participant

G · Test, Production, mobile app 10 points

  • Test every branch and calculation
  • Request Production
  • Mobile app: one offline record, synchronised

07Data Access Groups: the multi-centre part

New this year. One project, three sites; site staff see only their own site; data managers see everything.

  1. Create the groups. Applications > Data Access Groups: Dar es Salaam, Mwanza, Mbeya. Note the unique names REDCap makes (dar_es_salaam, …).
  2. Assign users. Each group member: one DAG and the Site Staff role. Keep tafadzwa and redcap in no DAG.
  3. Auto-numbering. Turn on record auto-numbering. New records get the DAG prefix, e.g. 2-1 for the first Mwanza participant.
  4. Enter data as a site user. Six fake participants per site, two per arm, with visits, one unscheduled visit and one missed visit.
  5. Import. Download the import template, add three participants with the column redcap_data_access_group, and import them.
  6. Prove the isolation. Screenshots of the Record Status Dashboard as a site user and as a data manager.
  7. Report and export by site. A report filtered by DAG; an export with the DAG column.

Answer in your report

Why does REDCap put the DAG number in front of the record ID? When would you choose separate projects per site instead of Data Access Groups?

10Questions

Can we build the instruments by uploading a data dictionary?

Yes. Many groups build one form in the Online Designer, download the dictionary, and write the rest in Excel. Upload, then check every form on screen.

The phone validation refuses Tanzanian numbers.

REDCap's phone validation is for North American numbers. Use a text field with @CHARLIMIT and a field note with the format, as asked in Part B.

Moving to Production needs an administrator. What if it is not approved before the deadline?

Request it early, a few working days before the deadline, and add a screenshot of your request to the report.

Can we use the TBCARE XML as a starting point?

You may look at it to see how features are built. Your TUMAINI project must be your own work, built from the TUMAINI CRFs.

Where do we ask questions?

In class or on the Blackboard discussion board, so that everybody gets the same answer.
